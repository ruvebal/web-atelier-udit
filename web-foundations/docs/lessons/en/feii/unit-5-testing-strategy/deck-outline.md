# Unit 5 deck outline — 1 h

1. FE I → FE II: tool literacy becomes test-suite judgement
2. Testing Trophy versus Pyramid: choose from the shape of the risk
3. The cost/confidence grid: what not to test
4. Flakiness is loss of trust, not an intermittent inconvenience
5. Four flake causes: time, order, network, animation/focus
6. Cypress → Playwright: defend the migration with a project constraint
7. CI wall-clock budget: measure, split, shard, fail early
8. MSW for fast feedback; contract tests for the boundary mocks cannot see
9. Versioned Unit 10 contract: how a field rename becomes a visible failure
10. Accessibility assertions are a floor; manual keyboard and screen-reader work remains
11. Handoff to Unit 6: an AI reviewer may comment on the suite, never decide for it

Speaker claims only. Framework and tool mechanics are platform notes; this deck makes no
unsupported claim that one testing sequence produces superior learning outcomes.
