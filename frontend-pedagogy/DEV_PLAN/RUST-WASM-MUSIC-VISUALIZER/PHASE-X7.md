## Goal

## Scope

## Acceptance

- [ ] All three sessions have actual delivery records; no future activity is marked complete by a planning agent.
- [ ] Each student's assessment includes individual evidence, not only a gallery artifact or team result.
- [ ] Public exhibition includes only approved material; copyrighted source music and private grade data are absent.
- [ ] Retrospective separates product defects, learning observations and untested hypotheses; next-iteration changes cite the corresponding evidence.
- [ ] Final report identifies what worked, what did not, and all deferred platform features.

## Risks

Ranking substituted for grades; missing individual authorship; claiming an efficacy study without a valid research design.
