## Student experience

1. **Orient:** state the professional problem and the success criterion.
2. **Make:** complete a small runnable artifact with a labelled template.
3. **Interrogate:** compare human reasoning with any AI suggestion; record accept/reject decisions.
4. **Prove:** submit individual evidence, not only a team product.
5. **Reflect:** name what the task does not establish and what would require further research.
